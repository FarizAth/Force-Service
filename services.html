Contractor Capabilities

Complete Floor Removal & Subfloor Preparation

We specialize in tearing out difficult flooring systems and restoring clean concrete subfloors for residential homes, retail stores, and commercial buildings.

1. Tile, Ceramic & Porcelain Removal

Removing stubborn ceramic, porcelain, slate, or stone tile in Central Florida homes requires mechanical leverage. Our heavy scraper shear blades slice right under the tile bed, lifting large sections cleanly.

  • Fast removal over large open living areas and commercial floors
  • Prevents gouging into structural concrete slab
  • Includes complete debris bag-out and disposal

2. Glued Hardwood & LVP Stripping

Glued engineered hardwood and luxury vinyl plank (LVP) can be a nightmare to scrape manually. Our specialized sharp machine edges pop glued wood planks right off the concrete slab without leaving splintered remnants.

  • Strips direct-glue wood flooring effortlessly
  • Removes glued sheet vinyl and resilient rubber tile

3. Thinset Mortar & Adhesive Grinding

Removing the top flooring is only half the job. New flooring cannot be installed over rigid thinset ridges or carpet glue. We perform thinset scraping and surface prep to leave your subfloor glass-smooth.

  • Flattens ridges for immediate luxury vinyl plank or wood layout
  • Removes sticky yellow mastic and cutback adhesive
